GC on Demand

For companies that need senior legal judgement on tap, but not every week.

GC on Demand is a lighter retainer engagement designed for earlier-stage companies or quieter operational periods. It gives you access to senior commercial legal judgement without the overhead of a full embedded engagement.

Typical scope

  • Contract review queue — inbound contracts reviewed and redlined within SLA
  • Monthly strategic check-in with leadership
  • Ad-hoc commercial negotiation support
  • Escalation path for material issues
  • Quarterly legal operations review
Who it's for
Series A to early Series B companies, or companies in a quieter phase between major commercial cycles.
Engagement shape
Retainer from €[Y]/month. Monthly rolling, three-month minimum.
