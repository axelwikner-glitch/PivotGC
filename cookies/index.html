Cookies. The short version.

This site sets one optional category of cookies — Google Ads conversion measurement — and nothing else. No cookies are set for that purpose unless you accept them.

01What we set

The complete list.

Your consent choice
Strictly necessary. Your accept/decline choice is stored in your browser's local storage under the key pgc-consent so we don't ask you on every visit. It never leaves your device and expires only when you clear your browser data.
Advertising (optional)
Google Ads conversion measurement. Only if you accept, Google's tag (gtag.js) sets cookies such as _gcl_au and _gcl_aw (typically expiring after 90 days) on this domain, and Google may set cookies on its own domains. These tell us whether a visit came from one of our ads and whether it led to a contact request. We use Google Consent Mode v2: until you accept, these cookies are not set and the tag operates without storing identifiers.
What we don't set
No analytics cookies, no social media pixels, no session cookies, no third-party trackers beyond the Google Ads tag above. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which serves files without setting cookies.

Change your mind any time. Your choice applies until you change it or clear your browser data.

For more on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's explanation. Questions about data on this site: info@pivotgc.com.
